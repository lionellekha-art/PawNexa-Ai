# 🐾 PawNexa AI

### AI-Powered Animal Rescue & Welfare Platform

PawNexa AI is a smart animal rescue platform that helps people quickly report animals in distress and helps rescue teams prioritize and respond to emergencies faster.

## 🚨 Features

* 🤖 **AI Emergency Priority Detection** – Identifies the urgency of animal cases.
* 📍 **Smart Location Detection** – Detects the user's location for rescue reporting.
* 🗣️ **Voice Reporting** – Allows users to report emergencies using voice.
* 📷 **Animal Photo Upload** – Upload photos of animals needing help.
* 🚑 **Smart Rescue Matching** – Suggests the most suitable rescue team.
* 🗺️ **AI Rescue Heatmap** – Identifies areas with repeated animal emergencies.
* 📊 **Live Rescue Dashboard** – Displays reported rescue cases and their priority.
* 🆘 **Emergency SOS Mode** – Provides quick access during critical situations.
* 📱 **Responsive Design** – Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## 💡 Problem

Animal emergencies are often reported late, and rescue teams may not receive enough information to determine which cases require immediate attention.

## 💡 Solution

PawNexa AI provides a centralized platform where users can report animals in distress. The system analyzes the reported condition, determines emergency priority, suggests rescue teams, and helps coordinate faster responses.
